# Ambient Clinical Analytics

> Rochester, Minnesota clinical decision support company spun out of Mayo Clinic in 2013. Its AWARE Patient Surveillance and AWARE Sepsis DART products are FDA Class II cleared, EMR-agnostic "bolt-on" dashboards that apply more than 1,200 licensed Mayo Clinic rules and algorithms to live patient data, delivering point-of-care visualization, situational awareness, and smart notifications for conditions such as sepsis across ICU, tele-ICU, and remote patient monitoring settings.

## API surface

Ambient Clinical Analytics publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no API documentation, no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK, no CLI, no MCP server, and no A2A agent card. No `api.`, `developer.`, `docs.`, `portal.`, or `app.` subdomain of ambientclinical.com resolves in DNS. Deployment into a hospital is described as EMR-agnostic and is performed by third-party data-integration partners rather than through a self-serve interface.

Do not attempt to call an Ambient Clinical Analytics API. Integration inquiries go through the contact form below.

## Products

- [AWARE Patient Surveillance](https://ambientclinical.com/aware-critical-care/): real-time patient monitoring and point-of-care data visualization; FDA Class II cleared for adult, pediatric, and neonatal patients.
- [AWARE Sepsis DART](https://ambientclinical.com/aware-sepsis-dart/): sepsis detection and response tracking, showing what care has been delivered, what is pending, and what is due before treatment windows close.
- [Sepsis solutions](https://ambientclinical.com/sepsis-solutions/): CMS SEP-1 reporting support.
- [Tele-ICU](https://ambientclinical.com/tele-icu/) and [remote patient monitoring](https://ambientclinical.com/remote-patient-monitoring/).
